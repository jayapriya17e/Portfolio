RF Signal Strength Robot

This project focuses on designing a robot capable of detecting RF signal strength and automatically moving toward stronger signals. The system uses a 433 MHz RF receiver connected to an AT89C51 microcontroller.

Objectives

  • Detect RF signal strength
  • Move robot toward stronger signal
  • Real-time signal tracking

Working

The wearable patch antenna consists of a radiating patch, substrate, and ground plane. When RF power is supplied through the feed line, current flows on the patch surface, generating electromagnetic radiation. The ground plane reflects the signal, improving radiation efficiency and gain. The antenna dimensions are optimized to resonate at 2.4154 GHz. The flexible substrate allows the antenna to conform to body surfaces. The antenna transmits wireless data from wearable sensors to nearby monitoring systems. Even when placed close to the human body, the antenna maintains stable performance. This design is suitable for medical monitoring devices, wireless body area networks, and portable communication systems.
